===============================================
SECTION 1: WHAT IS THIS SYSTEM?
===============================================

Dastaan Portal is a comprehensive web-based platform that manages the entire lifecycle of content development projects - from the moment a content creator pitches a story idea all the way through to contract execution and final payment processing.

The system provides:
- Complete visibility into project status at every stage
- Clear accountability with role-based access for different teams
- Structured evaluation and approval workflows
- Automated tracking and notifications
- Complete audit trail of all decisions and actions
- Centralized document and file management
- Real-time dashboards and analytics


WHY THIS SYSTEM IS NEEDED
--------------------------
Before this system, the organization faced several challenges:
• Scattered information across emails, spreadsheets, and documents
• No clear visibility into project status
• Unclear accountability ("Who's handling this project?")
• Manual follow-ups leading to delays
• Lost information during handoffs between departments
• Difficulty tracking contracts and payments
• No historical record of decisions
• Inconsistent evaluation criteria

Dastaan Portal solves all these problems by centralizing everything in one organized, secure, and transparent system.


===============================================
SECTION 2: THE COMPLETE 8-STAGE WORKFLOW
===============================================

The system models the complete content development lifecycle through 8 distinct stages:

STAGE 1: STORY SUBMISSION
-------------------------
Content creators submit story ideas with:
- Story title and synopsis
- Genre and target audience
- Writer/originator information
- Category (external producer, writer pitch, or in-house content)
- Suggested writers
- Supporting documents and attachments

Each story gets a unique ID (format: STR-2025-0001) for tracking.

STAGE 2: CALL REPORTS (MEETING DOCUMENTATION)
----------------------------------------------
Content managers document meetings and discussions:
- Meeting date, time, and attendees
- Writer contact information (name, email, phone, address)
- Working title and logline
- Unique Selling Proposition (USP)
- Target audience analysis
- Detailed meeting notes
- Next steps and action items
- File attachments (presentations, documents, etc.)

Each call report gets unique ID (format: CR-2025-0001).
Call reports can be for scheduled meetings or logged independently.

STAGE 3: EVALUATION
-------------------
Multiple evaluators independently review and score projects:

EVALUATION CRITERIA (1-10 scale):
1. Premise & Conflict - How compelling is the core premise?
2. Storyline & Plot - Quality of narrative structure
3. Episodic Progression - How well does the story unfold?
4. Characters - Character development and depth
5. Dialogues - Quality and authenticity of dialogue
6. Overall Assessment - General impression and viability

Additional Evaluation Fields:
- Producer name
- Genre classification
- Time slot recommendations
- Big idea summary
- Central theme
- Overall comments and recommendations
- First 2 episodes requirement (Yes/No)

Automatic average score calculation across all criteria.
System tracks completion status for each evaluator.
Multiple evaluators can be assigned to one project for diverse perspectives.

STAGE 4: APPROVAL (Planned - Database Ready)
---------------------------------------------
Executive approval workflow:
- One-liner summaries of projects
- Aggregated evaluation scores and feedback
- Price range recommendations
- Budget viability assessment
- Key strengths summary
- Urgency indicators
- Executive decisions: Approved/Rejected/Pending
- Decision notes and conditions
- Priority assignment

STAGE 5: NEGOTIATION (Planned - Database Ready)
------------------------------------------------
Price and terms negotiation tracking:
- Proposed pricing and payment structure
- Currency and payment terms
- Price justification documentation
- Counter offers with justifications
- Negotiation history log
- Expected start and completion dates
- Final agreed price and terms
- Failure tracking with reasons

STAGE 6: LEGAL REVIEW (Planned - Database Ready)
-------------------------------------------------
Legal compliance and contract review:
- Assigned legal reviewer
- Compliance checklist with progress tracking
- Risk assessment (Low/Medium/High)
- Legal concerns and recommendations
- Required modifications
- Approval conditions
- Decision: Approved/Rejected/Modifications Required
- Days in review tracking

STAGE 7: CONTRACT MANAGEMENT (Planned - Database Ready)
--------------------------------------------------------
Complete contract lifecycle management:
- Contract type and parties information
- Party A (Organization) representative
- Party B (Writer/Producer) details (NIC, NTN)
- Project title and description
- Contract duration (start/end dates)
- Total contract value and currency
- Payment terms and deliverables
- Milestone definitions
- Confidentiality clauses
- IP ownership terms
- Termination conditions
- Dispute resolution procedures
- Governing law
- Digital signature tracking (both parties)
- Contract status: Draft/Pending/Active/Completed/Terminated

STAGE 8: PAYMENT TRACKING (Planned - Database Ready)
-----------------------------------------------------
Milestone-based payment processing:
- Payment schedules linked to contracts
- Multiple milestone definitions
- Payment amounts and percentages
- Withholding tax calculations
- Net amount calculations
- Payment status: Pending/Approved/Paid/Rejected/Overdue
- Invoice management
- Bank account details
- Payment method tracking
- Approval workflow
- Overdue tracking with day counts
- Rejection reason documentation


ADDITIONAL SUPPORTING FEATURES (Database Ready)
------------------------------------------------

SCRIPT DEVELOPMENT TRACKING:
- 6-step script development process
- Episode and draft version tracking
- Page count, word count, scene count
- Writer notes and feedback
- Script file management
- Review deadlines and delay tracking

ARCHIVE SYSTEM:

WORKFLOW ENGINE:

7. FINANCE

Payment processors:
- View payment schedules
- Process milestone payments
